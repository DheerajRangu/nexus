# Provenance and licence note

All operational seed data (ambulances, hospitals, roadblocks, corridor signals, users) and all AI training samples are **synthetic**, generated by scripts in this repository (`backend` Flyway seeds, `ai-service/app/data/seed_generator.py`).

No real patient, caller, hospital census, or municipal traffic data is included.

Licence intent for hackathon deliverables: use only for demonstration and evaluation of the AEGIS prototype. Do not treat model metrics as validated clinical or operational performance.
